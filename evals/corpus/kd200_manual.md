# KD-200 Inspection Drone: Operator Manual

## Overview

The KD-200 is a quadcopter inspection drone with a 42 minute maximum flight time, a 1.2 kg payload capacity and an IP55 weather rating. It is designed for bridge, pipeline and wind-turbine inspection.

## Battery

The KD-200 uses the KB-9 smart battery pack (6S, 9,000 mAh). Batteries must be stored at 40 to 60 percent charge. A full charge takes 85 minutes with the standard KC-3 charger.

## Error Codes

| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| E-1042 | Compass interference detected | Move at least 10 metres away from metal structures and recalibrate the compass |
| E-2210 | Battery cell imbalance | Land immediately and replace the KB-9 battery pack |
| E-3005 | GPS signal lost | The drone switches to ATTI mode; fly manually and land as soon as possible |
| E-4100 | Motor overheating | Land and let motors cool for 15 minutes |

## Firmware

Firmware 3.8.1 added obstacle avoidance at speeds up to 12 m/s. Firmware updates are installed through the Kestrel Pilot app and take about 9 minutes.

## Maintenance

Propellers must be replaced every 120 flight hours or immediately after any visible damage. A full service inspection is required every 400 flight hours.
