# New Employee Onboarding Guide

## First Day

On your first day you will receive your laptop from the IT desk on floor 3 of the Rotterdam office. Your manager will schedule a welcome meeting at 10:00.

## First Week

During your first week you must complete the security awareness training and the data classification course in the Learning Hub. Engineers also complete the KD-200 safety briefing before visiting the test hangar.

## Buddy Program

Every new employee is paired with an onboarding buddy from another team for their first 90 days.

## Probation

The probation period is 3 months. Your manager will hold a probation review in week 11.

## Tools

Kestrel uses Slack for chat, Jira for issue tracking, Notion for documentation and Workday for HR processes.
