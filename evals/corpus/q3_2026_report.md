# Q3 2026 Quarterly Business Report

## Summary

Kestrel Dynamics reported Q3 2026 revenue of 48.6 million USD, up 22 percent year over year. Gross margin improved to 41 percent from 37 percent in Q3 2025, driven by lower KD-200 component costs.

## Revenue by Segment

| Segment | Q3 2026 revenue | Year-over-year growth |
|---|---|---|
| Inspection drones | 29.1 million USD | 31% |
| Software subscriptions | 12.4 million USD | 18% |
| Services | 7.1 million USD | 2% |

## Headcount

Headcount reached 612 employees at the end of Q3 2026, compared with 540 at the end of Q2 2026. Most new hires joined engineering and field services.

## Outlook

Management expects Q4 2026 revenue between 52 and 55 million USD. The KD-300 heavy-lift drone is scheduled to launch in February 2027.

## Risks

The main risks are supply constraints for flight controllers and new EU drone regulations that take effect in July 2027.
