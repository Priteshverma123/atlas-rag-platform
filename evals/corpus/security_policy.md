# Information Security Policy

Owner: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), Dana Okafor.

## Passwords and Authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all systems that contain customer data. Hardware security keys are required for administrators.

## Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident must be reported to security@kestrel.example within 4 hours of discovery. Do not attempt to investigate an incident yourself. Lost or stolen laptops must be reported within 1 hour.

## Data Classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted data, such as flight telemetry from defence customers, may only be stored in the EU-Frankfurt region and must never be copied to personal devices.

## Access Reviews

Managers review their team's system access every quarter. Access for departing employees is revoked on their last working day.
