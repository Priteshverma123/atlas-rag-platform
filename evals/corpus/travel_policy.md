# Kestrel Dynamics Travel and Expense Policy

Version 4.2, effective 1 March 2026. Owner: Finance Operations.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees and contractors of Kestrel Dynamics who travel on company business.

## Booking

All flights must be booked through the Navan travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class requires written approval from a Vice President.

## Daily Limits

The daily meal allowance is 75 USD in standard cities and 110 USD in high-cost cities such as London, Zurich, Tokyo and New York. Hotel rates are capped at 220 USD per night in standard cities and 340 USD per night in high-cost cities.

## Approvals

Trips costing more than 3,000 USD in total require pre-approval from the employee's director. International trips always require pre-approval regardless of cost.

## Reimbursement

Expense reports must be submitted in Expensify within 30 days of the trip ending. Receipts are required for any single expense above 25 USD. Reimbursements are paid in the next payroll cycle after approval.
